My 7-Day Trip

Introduction

I am planning a seven-day trip to Henan, visiting three cities: Zhengzhou, Luoyang, and Kaifeng. I will spend two days in Zhengzhou, two days in Luoyang, and three days in Kaifeng.

Daily Itinerary

  1. Day 1 - Exploring Zhengzhou

    On the first day, I plan to visit Henan Museum to learn more about Chinese history and culture. After visiting the museum, I would like to explore Zhenwei Water Town and enjoy its traditional-style buildings and beautiful scenery.

  2. Day 2 - Yinji Animal Kingdom

    I plan to spend the second day at Yinji Animal Kingdom, a popular wildlife theme park near Zhengzhou. I would like to see different animals and enjoy the attractions in the park.

  3. Day 3 - Discovering Luoyang

    On the third day, I plan to travel to Luoyang and visit the Longmen Grottoes, a UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for its Buddhist stone carvings. I would also like to explore Luoyang Museum to learn about the city's history.

  4. Day 4 - Ancient Luoyang

    I plan to visit Luoyi Ancient City and explore its traditional-style streets and buildings. In the evening, I would like to visit Yingtian Gate to enjoy the beautiful night views.

  5. Day 5 - Qingming Riverside Landscape Garden

    I plan to travel to Kaifeng and visit Qingming Riverside Landscape Garden. I would like to explore the traditional-style buildings, watch cultural performances, and experience the atmosphere of the Song Dynasty.

  6. Day 6 - Wansui Mountain Martial Arts City

    On the sixth day, I plan to spend the day at Wansui Mountain Martial Arts City. I am especially excited to watch traditional martial arts performances and experience the atmosphere of ancient Chinese stories.

  7. Day 7 - Daxiangguo Temple

    On the last day, I plan to visit Daxiangguo Temple, one of the famous Buddhist temples in Kaifeng. After exploring the temple, I would like to try some local food and enjoy my final moments in Henan before ending my trip.
